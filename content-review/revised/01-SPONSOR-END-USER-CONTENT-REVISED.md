## Main Narrative

### What is this?

Bharatiya Krishak Samaj Pujo is a seasonal Durga Puja that keeps worship, craft, food, music and neighbourhood at the centre — and then places the farmer, recognition and Integrated Farming inside that same public week.

It sits inside a wider ecosystem with specialised doors for sponsors, institutions, farmers, the public and NRB supporters. Sponsorship is the organisational door: a conversation about presence, partnership and platform — not a public booking desk.

### Why this Puja is different

Most festival sponsorship is inventory: a logo, a board, a few nights, a photograph. Decision-makers leave with visibility that ends when the immersion ends.

This Pujo asks a different question of an organisation:

1. **The Puja remains the gathering** — worship first, not an agriculture conference dressed as festival.
2. **The gathering names the farmer** — the annadata, including recognition for farmers Bengal does not usually photograph.
3. **Integrated Farming is shown as livelihood** — crop, animals, water and market on one holding, so leftover can become the next input.
4. **A live demonstration is being built** so the story can be walked, not only spoken.
5. **The public week sits beside a longer mobilisation ambition** — about 5,000 village integrated farms as a **target**, not a completed count.

The qualitative difference is presence at the start of a named platform. Reach, footfall, media impressions and ROI are not promised here.

### Beyond four days

The civic dates are four days: **16–20 October 2026**, Kolkata.

The proposition is a seasonal platform meant to continue beyond those nights. Detailed Year 2 and Year 3 programmes are not published here. Read the longer horizon as an invitation to a continuing conversation — not as a locked multi-year media plan.

### The story sponsors are entering

```text
Durga Puja (public week)
  → farmer visibility (annadata, recognition)
  → Integrated Farming (livelihood, not slogan)
  → public demonstration (live farm being built)
  → participation doors (sponsors, institutions, farmers, public, supporters)
  → longer agricultural conversation
```

Your organisation is being invited to consider presence at the beginning of that chain — not only decoration of a finished festival product.

### FarmTech + AgriTech relevance

FarmTech and AgriTech organisations may recognise a natural sector conversation: careful tools — soil moisture monitoring, drip, pond aeration, crop-health diagnostics, yield tracking — where they help a small Integrated Farming holding work more carefully.

That layer is a **model-based sketch** inside the farming story. This experience does not publish a vendor list or promise yields.
