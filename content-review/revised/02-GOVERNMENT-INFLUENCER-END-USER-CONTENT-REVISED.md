## Main Narrative

### What is the initiative?

Bharatiya Krishak Samaj Pujo is a seasonal Durga Puja in Kolkata that:

1. Keeps worship, craft, food, music and neighbourhood at the centre of the gathering.
2. Puts the farmer — the annadata — in public view, including recognition for farmers who are rarely photographed.
3. Shows Integrated Farming as a livelihood model on small land: multiple enterprises on one holding, with leftovers intended as inputs for the next activity.
4. Builds a live demonstration farm so institutions and the public can eventually look at the model on the ground.
5. Frames a mobilisation **target** of about **5,000** village integrated farms — a goal, not a completed count, and not a departmental target claimed here.

**Civic window:** 16–20 October 2026, Kolkata.  
**Exact Puja venue:** will be announced through official Puja channels.

### Why the farmer matters

A public festival week is when attention is shared across neighbourhoods. This Pujo uses that attention to name the person who grows food — so agricultural livelihood stays inside the civic conversation.

Recognition on Ashtami evening for unnamed farmers, and open nominations with no entry fee on the public surface, are part of that visibility. Visibility prepares understanding. The continuing work is demonstration and carefully labelled mobilisation.

### Integrated Farming

In plain institutional language:

An Integrated Farming System holds more than one enterprise on the same land — for example crop, horticulture, livestock, poultry, fishery / aquaculture, water harvesting, compost and soil work, with allied activities such as bee and mushroom where they fit. Residue, manure, pond silt and water can support other activities on the same holding. Household and market needs can be served together.

Presented here as a **model** for public understanding — not as a notified government scheme, and not as a guaranteed income programme.

Where farming materials mention illustrative cash-flow or income-range sketches, those remain **illustrative / model-based**. They are not upgraded to policy claims.

### FarmTech + AgriTech

FarmTech and AgriTech names the careful-tools layer of the model: soil moisture monitoring, drip irrigation, pond aeration, crop-health diagnostics, yield tracking and related tools where they help a small holding work more carefully.

This is not a vendor catalogue. This is not a claim of statewide digital agriculture deployment. Keep the language model-based and modest.

## Why It Matters

From an institutional seat, relevance is practical:

- A public week that names farmer livelihood without turning the Puja into a party platform.
- A model that can be inspected later on a live site.
- A mobilisation ambition stated as a target, so scale is visible without false completion.
- Separate doors for sponsors, farmers, visitors and supporters — so roles do not collapse into one fundraising message.

West Bengal’s agrarian structure is shaped heavily by small and marginal holdings. A livelihood model designed for small land is therefore relevant to any institution that cares about rural resilience — without converting that context into a BKS achievement claim.

### How the pieces relate (causal chain)

Institutions often ask whether the farming story is an insert or a consequence. The intended sequence is explicit:

```text
Public Puja week
  → farmer named and recognised
  → livelihood question (Integrated Farming)
  → demonstration being built for inspection
  → mobilisation target stated carefully
  → briefing / conversation for institutions who wish to look
```

Each step follows from the last. The Puja is not converted into a policy seminar. Agriculture is not dropped in as unrelated decoration.

### What “looking” can mean

Looking can mean:

- reading this briefing with your team
- requesting a conversation
- visiting or reviewing the demonstration as it becomes inspectable
- asking what is confirmed versus proposed before any further step

Looking does **not** mean you have endorsed the initiative. It does not mean a partnership exists. It does not mean 294 seats are partners.
