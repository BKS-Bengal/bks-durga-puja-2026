## Main Narrative

### Belonging

Belonging is not only geography. Many Bengalis carry a native village in memory while living in another city or another country. Durga Puja is often the season when that memory becomes loud — homecoming, even when the body cannot travel.

Across the diaspora, Puja has long been a way to rebuild a home away from home: shared rituals, shared food, shared evenings, children learning what the week means. This experience honours that feeling — and then asks a further question: can your name also sit beside a village farming story that continues after immersion?

You do not need to pretend you are standing in the pandal every evening. You are invited to stay connected in a credible way.

### Why Bengal still matters

Bengal’s public week is cultural. Bengal’s quiet week is agricultural: land, water, labour, price, season. Small and marginal holdings carry much of that work. When a festival names the farmer, it is not nostalgia alone — it is attention returned to the people who feed the household.

You do not need a lecture to feel that. You need a clear path to participate without false payment promises.

### The Puja as an entry point

Bharatiya Krishak Samaj Pujo is the public meeting-place: neighbourhood, clay, dhak, food, homecoming. Civic window: **16–20 October 2026**, Kolkata. Exact pandal venue will be announced through official Puja channels.

You need not stand in the pandal to express interest in the farming story. The Puja is the season when attention is shared; the farm is the work meant to continue.

Historical / reference photographs from earlier gatherings (including 2025 craft and community images) show previous work and atmosphere. They are not presented as the 2026 pandal unless clearly captioned.

### The farmer

The gathering names the annadata. Recognition on Ashtami evening honours farmers Bengal does not usually photograph. Nominations are open on the public surface with no entry fee.

A supporter’s interest does not replace the local team. Mobilisation language on file is that local teams of about **2–3 progressive farmers or FPOs** still put in land, labour and working capital. Your proposed seed is meant to sit **beside** that work — not instead of it.

### Integrated Farming

The farm you would be interested in seeding is meant to be an Integrated Farming holding: crop, horticulture, livestock, poultry, fishery / aquaculture, water harvesting, compost, soil, and allied livelihoods such as bee or mushroom where they fit — so leftover can become input.

This is livelihood design, not a slogan. Deeper enterprise detail lives on the FarmTech + AgriTech / Farmer door. Illustrative income sketches elsewhere remain **model-based**, not guarantees for any farm you may later follow.

### FarmTech + AgriTech

FarmTech and AgriTech names careful tools inside that farm — soil moisture monitoring, drip, pond aeration, crop-health diagnostics, yield tracking — where they help. Model-based. Not a software marketplace. Not a claim that every supporter receives a finished gadget kit through this experience.

## FAQ

### I live outside Bengal. Can I still take part?
Yes. This door is for Non-Resident Bengalis and supporters, including those abroad and those in Indian cities with a native village in Bengal.

### Do I have to be in the pandal?
No. The Puja is the public entry season. Interest can be expressed from wherever you are.

### Why does the farmer matter if I am far away?
Because the household you remember is still fed by someone’s land and labour. This Pujo names that person and shows a livelihood model worth seeding carefully.

### What is Integrated Farming here?
A multi-enterprise village farm where leftovers can become inputs. The farm you may later help seed is meant to hold that loop.

### What does ₹1 lakh mean?
It is **proposed** seed-support for one village integrated farm — in full, or ₹20,000 every two months. It is not collected through this experience.

### What does ₹20,000 every two months mean?
An alternative proposed pattern for the same seed-support idea across the year — still proposed, still not collected here.

### Are 5,000 patrons and 5,000 farms already achieved?
No. Both are mobilisation **targets**.

### What does ₹50 crore mean?
It is the arithmetic of the target (5,000 × ₹1 lakh). It is not funds already raised.

### Will you allocate a farm to me automatically?
No. There is no live matching or allocation through this experience.

### Can I pay by UPI or card here? Is 80G available here?
No. Payment and 80G are not available through this experience.

### What does Express Supporter Interest do?
Downloads an interest note to your device. You may email it to continue the conversation. It does not take money or confirm a farm.

### Who organises this Pujo?
Organised by KarmYog for the 21st Century. Organising partner: Bharatiya Krishak Samaj.

### What is FarmTech + AgriTech in this story?
Careful tools inside the Integrated Farm. Model-based. Not a product store.

### Where is the demonstration?
Munshir Bheri Management / Fishermen’s Committee, Near Sukantanagar / Salt Lake Sector V, East Kolkata Wetlands, Kolkata – 700091, West Bengal — being built.

### How do I stay connected after expressing interest?
Keep your downloaded note, email it if you wish, and watch for later approved contribution and pairing channels.
