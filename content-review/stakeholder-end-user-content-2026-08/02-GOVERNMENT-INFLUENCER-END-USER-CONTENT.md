## What Is the Initiative?

Bharatiya Krishak Samaj Pujo is a seasonal Durga Puja in Kolkata that:

1. Keeps worship, craft, food, music and neighbourhood at the centre of the gathering.
2. Puts the farmer — the annadata — in public view, including recognition for farmers who are rarely photographed.
3. Shows Integrated Farming as a livelihood model on small land: multiple enterprises on one holding, with leftovers intended as inputs for the next activity.
4. Builds a live demonstration farm in the East Kolkata Wetlands context (named place on file: Munshir Bheri Management / Fishermen’s Committee, near Sukantanagar / Salt Lake Sector V, Kolkata 700091). Status: **being built**. Exact Puja pandal: **to be announced**.
5. Frames a mobilisation **target** of about **5,000** village integrated farms — a goal, not a completed count, and not a departmental target claimed on this page.

**Civic window:** 16–20 October 2026, Kolkata.

## Why the Farmer Matters

A public festival week is when attention is shared across neighbourhoods. This Pujo uses that attention to name the person who grows food — so agricultural livelihood is not left outside the civic conversation.

Recognition on Ashtami evening for unnamed farmers, and open nominations with no entry fee on the public surface, are part of that visibility. Visibility is not the same as a finished farm programme. The farm work continues through demonstration and mobilisation language that stays carefully labelled.

---

## Integrated Farming

In plain institutional language:

An Integrated Farming System holds more than one enterprise on the same land — for example crop, horticulture, livestock, poultry, fishery / aquaculture, water harvesting, compost and soil work, with allied activities such as bee and mushroom where they fit. The working idea is that residue, manure, pond silt and water can support other activities on the same holding, and that household and market needs can be served together.

This page presents Integrated Farming as a **model** shown in public — not as a notified government scheme, and not as a guaranteed income programme.

Illustrative / model-based figures that appear in project farming material (for example 365-day cash-flow sketches, or 3×–5× income ranges) are **not** guarantees and are **not** upgraded here to policy claims.

---

## FarmTech + AgriTech

FarmTech and AgriTech names the careful-tools layer of the model: monitoring, drip irrigation, pond energy, diagnostics and related tools where they help a small holding work more carefully.

This is **not** a vendor catalogue. It is **not** a claim of statewide digital agriculture deployment. A fuller public definition of FarmTech and AgriTech as a named programme remains to be confirmed by stakeholders. Until then, keep the language model-based and modest.
