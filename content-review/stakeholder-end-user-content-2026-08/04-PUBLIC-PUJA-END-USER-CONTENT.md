## The Puja

This Sharadiya, the Puja is the public meeting-place: neighbourhood, clay, dhak, food, homecoming. Neighbours in Kolkata and Bengalis living away from Bengal can enter the gathering here.

Bharatiya Krishak Samaj Pujo is not inventing agriculture as a decorative theme so much as returning attention to soil, seed, water and the farming family inside a festival that already carries harvest memory — Nabapatrika, Kola Bou, the seasonal return.

---

## Why This Puja

Many Kolkata Pujas stage cinema, architecture, politics or spectacle. This one places the farmer — the annadata — in the gathering, then shows Integrated Farming as a livelihood people can understand, and points to a live demonstration being built.

The emotional centre remains the Puja. The farming story follows from the gathering; it does not replace it.

## Neighbourhood / Community

Durga Puja in Kolkata is famously public: crowds walking together, class and neighbourhood lines softening for a few nights of shared looking. That civic character is why a farmer-centred story can sit inside the week without becoming a closed conference.

---

## Farmer

The gathering names the farmer who grows food, so the person who feeds the household is seen — not left unnamed.

**Recognition:** an award ceremony on Ashtami evening recognises the unnamed farmer — people Bengal does not usually photograph. Nominations are open on the public surface. There is **no entry fee**.

You may nominate a farmer you know, or nominate yourself if you are the farmer. Scale is not the only test: a small holding with a genuinely better method matters here.

---

## Integrated Farming

Once the farmer is in view, the next question is livelihood. Integrated Farming keeps crop, animals, water and market on one holding, so leftover can become input — a model a small farm can run.

This public page keeps that explanation short. The deeper farm model lives on the FarmTech + AgriTech / Farmer door. Do not expect this Puja page to become an agriculture textbook.

## The Larger Story

Six steps, each following the last:

1. **The Puja** — a gathering people can join.
2. **The farmer** — the annadata comes into view.
3. **Recognition** — awards for the unnamed farmer.
4. **Livelihood** — why Integrated Farming follows.
5. **Demonstration** — a live farm being built (East Kolkata Wetlands context; Munshir Bheri Management / Fishermen’s Committee area near Sukantanagar / Salt Lake Sector V, Kolkata 700091).
6. **The seed** — this Puja is also the public beginning of a mobilisation **target** of about 5,000 integrated farms. ₹1 lakh is **proposed** seed for one village farm — or ₹20,000 every two months — with one NRB / supporter patron each, from Puja 2026 to Puja 2027. That is a goal, not money already collected. Nothing on this page takes money.
