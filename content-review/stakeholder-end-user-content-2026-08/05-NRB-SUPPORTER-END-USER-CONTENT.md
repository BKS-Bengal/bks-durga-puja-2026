## Belonging

Belonging is not only geography. Many Bengalis carry a native village in memory while living in another city or another country. Durga Puja is often the season when that memory becomes loud — homecoming, even when the body cannot travel.

This experience does not ask you to pretend you are standing in the pandal every evening. It asks whether you want your name beside a village farming story that continues after the immersion.

---

## Why Bengal Still Matters

Bengal’s public week is cultural. Bengal’s quiet week is agricultural: land, water, labour, price, season. Small and marginal holdings carry much of that work. When a festival names the farmer, it is not nostalgia alone — it is attention returned to the people who feed the household.

You do not need a lecture on statistics to feel that. You need a clear path to participate without false payment promises.

## The Farmer

The gathering names the annadata. Recognition on Ashtami evening honours farmers Bengal does not usually photograph. Nominations are open on the public surface with no entry fee.

A supporter’s interest does not replace the local team. The mobilisation language on file is that local teams of about **2–3 progressive farmers or FPOs** still put in land, labour and working capital. Your proposed seed is meant to sit **beside** that work — not instead of it.

---

## Integrated Farming

The farm you would be interested in seeding is meant to be an Integrated Farming holding: crop, horticulture, livestock, poultry, fishery / aquaculture, water harvesting, compost, soil, and allied livelihoods such as bee or mushroom where they fit — so leftover can become input.

This is livelihood design, not a slogan. Deeper enterprise detail lives on the FarmTech + AgriTech / Farmer door. Illustrative income sketches elsewhere remain **model-based**, not guarantees for any farm you may later follow.

---

## FarmTech + AgriTech

FarmTech and AgriTech names careful tools inside that farm — monitoring, drip, pond energy, diagnostics — where they help. Model-based. Not a software marketplace. Not Palm Tech. Not a claim that every patron receives a finished gadget kit from this website.

## How Supporters Can Participate

What you can do now:

1. Read the story carefully — Puja, farmer, Integrated Farming, target language.
2. **Express Supporter Interest** — leave your name, contact, and any ancestral district you wish to name.

## FAQ

### I live outside Bengal. Can I still take part?
Yes. This door is for Non-Resident Bengalis and supporters, including those abroad and those in Indian cities with a native village in Bengal.

### Do I have to be in the pandal?
No. The Puja is the public entry season. Interest can be expressed from wherever you are.

### Why does the farmer matter to me if I am far away?
Because the household you remember is still fed by someone’s land and labour. This Pujo names that person and shows a livelihood model worth seeding carefully.

### What is Integrated Farming here?
A multi-enterprise village farm where leftovers can become inputs. The farm you may later help seed is meant to hold that loop — not a single slogan crop.

### Is ₹1 lakh required? Is it collected here?
₹1 lakh (or ₹20,000 every two months) is **proposed** seed-support. It is **not** collected on this website.

### Are 5,000 patrons and 5,000 farms already achieved?
No. Both are mobilisation **targets**.

### Will you allocate a farm to me automatically?
No. There is no live matching or allocation engine on this page.

### Can I pay by UPI or card here? Is 80G available here?
No. Payment and 80G are not live on this page.

### What does Express Supporter Interest actually do?
Downloads a local JSON file. Does not take money. Does not confirm a farm. You may email the file yourself.

### Who organises this Pujo?
Organised by KarmYog for the 21st Century. Organising partner: Bharatiya Krishak Samaj.

### What is FarmTech and AgriTech in this story?
Careful tools inside the Integrated Farm. Model-based. Not a product store.

### How do I stay connected after expressing interest?
Keep your downloaded note, email it if you wish, and watch for later approved contribution and pairing channels — off this page.
