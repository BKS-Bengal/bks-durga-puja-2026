## Why This Puja Is Different

Most festival sponsorship is inventory: a few nights, a logo, a board. Decision-makers often leave with a photograph and little else.

This Pujo is built around a different public argument:

1. **The Puja remains the gathering** — worship, craft, food, music, neighbourhood. It does not rewrite the festival as an agriculture conference.
2. **The gathering names the farmer** — the annadata, the person who feeds the household — including recognition for farmers Bengal does not usually photograph.
3. **Integrated Farming is shown as livelihood** — crop, animals, water and market on one holding, so leftover can become the next input.
4. **A live demonstration is being built** at Munshir Bheri Management / Fishermen’s Committee, near Sukantanagar / Salt Lake Sector V, East Kolkata Wetlands, Kolkata 700091. That place is on file for the wetland / fishermen’s-committee site. The exact Puja pandal remains to be announced.
5. **The public week sits beside a longer mobilisation ambition** — about 5,000 village integrated farms as a **target**, not a completed count.

What this does **not** promise: guaranteed footfall, guaranteed media reach, guaranteed ROI, or government endorsement.

The intended setting is decision-makers and the public in one living week, with the farmer and Integrated Farming at the centre. That is the qualitative difference. It is not a rate card for four nights alone.
