## A. SOURCE FACT

### What integrated farming is

Integrated farming systems (IFS) combine enterprises — crop, livestock, aquaculture, trees, composting, sometimes biogas, poultry, apiary — so that the residue of one is an input for another. ICAR describes this as a resource-saving design, especially for small and marginal farms. Waste is treated as a resource in the wrong place, not as rubbish. [SRC-IFS-001] [SRC-IFS-002]

This is a **national research concept**. It is not a BKS trademark and not a list of BKS farms.

### West Bengal’s holding pattern

Bidhan Chandra Krishi Viswavidyalaya (BCKV), the state agricultural university running AICRP on IFS, states that small and marginal farmers are about **96%** of farmers in West Bengal. [SRC-WB-001] Trade-press reporting of NABARD West Bengal remarks uses the same share and names weak post-harvest and market orientation as the problem FPOs are meant to ease. [SRC-FPO-002]

Do not cite the all-India 86.21% small+marginal figure as a West Bengal number. [SRC-CENSUS-001]

### Crop, animals, water

Crop plus livestock is the common Indian mix; “integration” means the mix is planned (fodder, dung, milk, labour), not two separate struggles. [SRC-IFS-003] [SRC-IFS-004]

In Bengal, water is part of the farm. Rice–fish in seasonally flooded fields has been the subject of ICAR-CIFRI work with state departments. [SRC-AQ-001] CIFRI also documents backyard pond culture in South 24 Parganas, including programmes with women participants. Those people are **not** BKS testimonials. [SRC-AQ-002]

BCKV’s AICRP page describes **raised and sunken beds** for low Gangetic land: vegetables on the raised bed, rice and fish in the sunken bed in the rains. That is a published university / AICRP design, not a BKS invention. [SRC-WB-001]

### One published on-station model (not a BKS farm)

BCKV records an on-station **0.66 ha** model at Gayeshpur (from 2012): crop 0.43 ha, horticulture 0.11 ha, dairy / vermicompost / biogas 0.03 ha, fishery 0.09 ha. [SRC-WB-001]  
A Nadia comparison of six IFS models versus a traditional system is **one study**, not a statewide average or a BKS result. [SRC-WB-003]

A named 3.0 ha farm example on a BCKV page (Shantipur block, Nadia) is an institutional case study. It must not be presented as a BKS farmer story. [SRC-WB-004]

### Markets, storage, collectives

Public policy already points smallholders to Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) for aggregation and markets. [SRC-FPO-002] [SRC-FPO-003]  
BKS can **explain** that idea on a knowledge page. This seasonal site does not operate a warehouse, shop, or FPO.

### Beyond the plot

West Bengal Homestay Tourism Policy, 2022, encourages rural / eco / cultural tourism and mentions dovetailing organic farming with tourism packages. There is no evidence here of a single dedicated “Agro-tourism Policy” by that name. Agro-tourism is an **ecosystem possibility**, not a BKS product. [SRC-TOUR-001] [SRC-TOUR-002]

WBSRLM / Anandadhara describes Integrated Farming Clusters and women farm cadres (including Progressive Mahila Kisan / Prani Mitra). That is **government livelihoods architecture**, not a BKS programme. [SRC-GOV-002]

Nadia KVK (ICAR-sponsored, under BCKV) is a public extension window. Knowledge content should point toward such windows, not replace them. [SRC-WB-005]

State agriculture works alongside animal resources, fisheries, marketing, horticulture and cooperation. The official crop-solution channel is Matir Katha. This site must not impersonate it. [SRC-GOV-001]
