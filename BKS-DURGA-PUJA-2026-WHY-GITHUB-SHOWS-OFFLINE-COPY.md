## What “offline copy” means here

| Phrase | Actual meaning in this repo |
|---|---|
| Offline | Static files + bundled JSON. No CMS. Forms save JSON on the user’s device. No payment gateway. |
| Copy | Isolated seasonal Puja 2026 experience. Not the live chapter site. |
| Not production (README) | Not `bkswbengal.org`. Do not mix with Amul/GOBARdhan, Purulia, OmniSocial, or other chapter production. |

Technical facts:

1. Implementation is `site/` — HTML, CSS, JS, hash routes.
2. Content is bundled in `site/scripts/offline-data.js`.
3. Local open path: `python -m http.server 8780` from `site/`, or `OPEN-OFFLINE.bat` (opens `site/index.html` on the machine).
4. Isolation rules in `ISOLATION.md` keep this workstream off live `bkswbengal.org` and other production properties.
