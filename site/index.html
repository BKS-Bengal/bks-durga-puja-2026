Cultural context first

The Puja

Worship, craft, food, music and neighbourhood. Festival facts are separated from what is not yet true of a BKS gathering.

This page uses three labels: SOURCE FACT (the festival in Bengal), BKS POSITIONING (where the Samaj sits), and PENDING INFORMATION (what is not yet true of a BKS gathering).

Source fact — the festival in Bengal

SOURCE FACT VERIFIED In December 2021, ‘Durga Puja in Kolkata’ was inscribed on UNESCO’s Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. The inscribed element is Kolkata’s Puja — not this page, and not an unnamed venue.

SOURCE FACT VERIFIED Images are unfired clay, traditionally from the Ganga, made in artisanal workshops. Eyes are painted from Mahalaya. The image returns to water on the tenth day. That is the festival’s material fact, not a BKS workshop.

SOURCE FACT VERIFIED Traditional Bengali drumming is named in the UNESCO inscription. Dhakis keep that sound. No unnamed drummer is exhibited here, and this page does not autoplay audio.

SOURCE FACT RESEARCH-DERIVED Household (bonedi bari) Pujas and public barowari / sarbojanin Pujas coexist. A city theme-pandal is not the same as a district neighbourhood gathering. BKS will not use those committee words until a real committee chooses them.

SOURCE FACT RESEARCH-DERIVED Bhog is food offered and then shared. Community kitchens often serve khichuri, mixed vegetables, fries, chutney and payesh. Whether BKS will serve bhog is not decided here.

The public days — cultural sequence, not a BKS timetable

Meanings below are research-derived. Gregorian clocks for 2026 are not printed as ritual fact.

  • RESEARCH-DERIVED

    Mahalaya

    Pitru Paksha ends. In Bengal this dawn is also cultural memory: the goddess is turned toward the living year.

  • RESEARCH-DERIVED

    Shashthi

    Bodhon — the public awakening of the image.

  • RESEARCH-DERIVED

    Saptami

    Nabapatrika / Kolabou. Full public worship typically begins.

  • RESEARCH-DERIVED

    Ashtami

    Pushpanjali, Kumari Puja, and Sandhi Puja at the join of Ashtami and Navami.

  • RESEARCH-DERIVED

    Navami

    Culminating worship in many committees.

  • RESEARCH-DERIVED

    Dashami

    Sindoor Khela, Bijoya, and immersion — often on a civil day also fixed with the police.
