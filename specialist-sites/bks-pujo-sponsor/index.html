Why this is different

Most festival sponsorship is inventory: a logo, a board, a few nights, a photograph. The association ends when the lights come down.

This Pujo asks a different question of an organisation. The Puja remains the gathering. The gathering names the farmer. Integrated Farming is shown as livelihood. A live demonstration is being built at the Puja venue. The public week sits beside a longer invitation: about 5,000 village integrated farms as a vision to work toward, not a count already reached.

The qualitative difference is presence at the start of a named platform. Reach, footfall, media impressions and ROI are not promised here. The next step is a conversation. Nothing on this page takes money.

The story you are entering

  1. The Puja

    The public week. Worship first, not an agriculture conference dressed as festival.

  2. Farmer visibility

    The annadata is named and recognised, including farmers Bengal does not usually photograph.

  3. Integrated Farming

    Livelihood, not slogan: leftover becomes input, on a holding people can understand.

  4. Demonstration

    A live farm at the venue, so the story can be walked.

  5. Participation

    Sponsors, institutions, farmers, public, supporters.

  6. A longer conversation

    Agricultural work that continues beyond the civic nights.

Not just four days

The civic dates are four days. The invitation is to a seasonal platform meant to continue. Later-year programmes are not published here. About 5,000 village farms is a vision to work toward. Rs 1 lakh is proposed seed for one village farm, or Rs 20,000 every two months. That arithmetic is a horizon, not money already raised, and it is not collected here.

Title association

Bharatiya Krishak Samaj is the organising partner. It does not occupy the title-partner place. No title partner is named on this experience. Organisations ready to discuss title or category association are invited to begin a conversation.

FarmTech + AgriTech

FarmTech and AgriTech organisations may recognise a natural sector conversation: careful tools where they help a small Integrated Farming holding. That layer is a model-based sketch. This experience does not publish a vendor list or promise yields.

Previous Puja

Durga Puja Mahotsav 2025 was organised by KarmYog for the 21st Century Foundation at the IIT Kharagpur Research Park in New Town, Kolkata. It was inaugurated on Mahalaya by Dr Sukanta Majumdar, Union Minister of State for Education, and Prof Suman Chakraborty, Director, IIT Kharagpur. Photographs from that gathering are last year's craft and atmosphere. They are not photographs of the 2026 Bharatiya Krishak Samaj pandal unless a caption says so.

Questions a board will ask

What does sponsorship mean in the context of this Puja?

Presence with a named public gathering that keeps worship at the centre and places the farmer and Integrated Farming in view. A platform conversation, not a four-night hoarding purchase.

The civic window is four days. The invitation is to a seasonal platform meant to continue.

Who is this opportunity intended for?

Organisations that want cultural association with meaning, including agriculture, farm inputs, FarmTech + AgriTech, and community-minded brands.
