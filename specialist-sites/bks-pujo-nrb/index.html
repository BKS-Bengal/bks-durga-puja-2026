You can stand with a village farm without standing in the village every day.

The working definition already on this project: a Non-Resident Bengali is anyone with roots in a native village. That includes people who live abroad, people who live in other Indian cities, and people who live in a Kolkata high-rise. This is not a visa category and not a legal NRI product.

The role is patronage of a named farm when pairing is real, not a souvenir from a pandal. Pairing is intended later. It is not a live matching workflow on this website.

The festival is the public week. You need not stand in the pandal to take part.

Bharatiya Krishak Samaj Pujo is a seasonal gathering in Kolkata. Civic dates on this project are 16-20 October 2026. The exact pandal address is still to be announced.

That week is when the farmer, the award, and the live farm can be seen in public. Belonging is not geography-only. Interest can be expressed from wherever you are.

The person who grows the food should not stay unnamed.

Recognition, including Ashtami awards, is how this Pujo insists the farmer is part of the gathering. Patronage is meant to sit beside a local farmer team that still puts in land, labour, and working capital.

One farm, one patron is the intended model. Bharatiya Krishak Samaj, West Bengal is meant to pair a supporter with a local team later. This page does not allocate a farm, name a village for you, or confirm an adoption.

Intended, not live

A named farm beside a local team. Not a match made by this website. Not a souvenir from the festival week.

The farm you would seed is a loop, not a slogan.

An Integrated Farming System keeps several enterprises on one small holding. Leftover from one part becomes input for the next. Mix, scale, and results vary. This page does not enrol a farm.

  • Water and fish

    A pond as harvest and reservoir, not a decorative tank.

  • Bund horticulture

    Raised edges planted so the water line also feeds fruit and greens.

  • Livestock beside the pond

    Animals and birds whose leftover returns as feed, slurry, or compost.

  • Soil from the farm itself

    Compost and bio-inputs made on the holding, so bought chemicals are not the whole story.

A farm you may eventually follow. The picture is not finished.

A live integrated-farm demonstration is being built in the East Kolkata Wetlands, 500 metres from Sector V. It is intended as a working pavilion, not only a festive facade. Whether a dump-yard story belongs to this plot is pending confirmation.

Photographs of a completed transformation are not published here. A later portal to follow a farm from afar is future work. There is no login on this website.

Mobilisation target, not a headcount

5,000 patrons beside 5,000 farms.

That pairing is the mobilisation already published on this project, from Puja 2026 toward Puja 2027. It is a goal. It is not a claim that 5,000 farms or 5,000 patrons already exist.

Rs 1 lakh is the proposed seed for one village farm, or Rs 20,000 every two months. Those figures are proposed. They are not collected on this page. They are not a guaranteed grant.

Questions people ask from far away

Who is this for?

Non-Resident Bengalis and supporters outside Bengal, and India-based supporters with a native village in Bengal. The public label is Supporter / NRB. It is not a passport test.

Why should NRBs take part?

If your native village is in Bengal, this Pujo is a way to take part in Bengal’s farming story from wherever you are. Belonging is not only a seat in the pandal.

What does participation mean on this page?

You express interest in seeding a village integrated farm. The form downloads a file to your device. Pairing with a local farmer team is intended later. This is not live allocation, adoption, or payment.

How does this connect to the farmer?

The festival names the farmer, including recognition such as Ashtami awards. Patronage is meant to sit beside a local team that still puts in land, labour, and working capital.

What is the larger vision?

A closed-loop village farm, shown as a demonstration being built, and as a model that about 5,000 patrons might later seed beside about 5,000 farms. That scale is a target.

Have 5,000 supporters already joined?

No. About 5,000 patrons and about 5,000 farms is a mobilisation target, not a completed count.

Can I pay on this page?

No. There is no UPI, card, gateway, or cash collection here. Rs 1 lakh, or Rs 20,000 every two months, is proposed seed-support, not money taken on this website.

What happens after I download the file?

The file stays on your device. This website does not upload it. You may send bks-pujo-nrb-interest.json to Bharatiya Krishak Samaj separately, for example at contact@bkswbengal.org. Sending it is your choice.

Are 80G or tax documents available?

To be confirmed. They are not issued on this website. Any tax pathway would be confirmed only if a contribution is later completed off this website.

What is confirmed, and what is only proposed?

Confirmed here: organised by KarmYog for the 21st Century; Bharatiya Krishak Samaj is the organising partner, not the title sponsor; civic dates 16-20 October 2026 in Kolkata; venue to be announced; this page takes no payment; the form only downloads a local file. Proposed or pending: the 5,000 pairing, the seed amount, live matching, 80G, transformation photographs, and a follow-from-afar portal.
