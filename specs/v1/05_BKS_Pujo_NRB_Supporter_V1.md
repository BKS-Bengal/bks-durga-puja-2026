# 1. Document Purpose

Specify the NRB / supporter experience so someone far from the village — or in a city apartment — understands how this Pujo can be a way to take part in Bengal’s farming story, what support **means**, what is **proposed**, and what is **not live** (payment, allocation, matching, ₹1 lakh receipt).

# 5. V1 Objective

The reader must understand:

1. Belonging is not geography-only; native village roots are the working definition on file.
2. The Puja is the public week; patronage is meant to sit beside a local farmer team (land, labour, working capital) — **INTENDED** pairing.
3. About 5,000 patrons and 5,000 farms is a **TARGET**, not a completed count.
4. ₹1 lakh (or ₹20,000 every two months) is **PROPOSED** seed-support, not money collected here.
5. Express interest = JSON download. No UPI, card, allocation, matching engine, or automatic confirmation.
6. A later portal / off-site contribution is **FUTURE / NOT LIVE** if mentioned at all.

# 10. Section-by-Section Content Architecture

### 10.1 Why NRBs / supporters

Working definition on file. Role is patronage of a named farm **when pairing is real**, not a souvenir from a pandal. Pairing is **NOT YET AVAILABLE** as a live workflow.

### 10.2 The Puja as the entry

Seasonal gathering in Kolkata. Farmer, award, live farm can be seen in public. You do not have to be in the pandal to express interest.

### 10.3 The annadata

Ashtami awards; patronage beside a local team.

### 10.4 How it works

₹1 lakh proposed seed, or ₹20,000 every two months. One farm, one patron — **INTENDED**. BKS West Bengal meant to pair later. UPI, cards, tax paperwork **off this page when an adoption is real**. Form only registers interest as a downloaded file.

### 10.5 Collective vision

TARGET: about 5,000 patrons · about 5,000 farms. From Puja 2026 toward Puja 2027 as already published. Not a claim that they already exist.

### 10.6 Live farm / transformation

EKW 500 m from Sector V, being built. Whether dump-yard story is this plot: **PENDING CONFIRMATION**. Follow-from-afar portal: **FUTURE / NOT LIVE** if mentioned (existing NRB FAQ mentions a later digital portal — keep as future, not a login).
