# 1. Document Purpose

Specify the Farmer / IFS V1 experience so a farmer (or a serious IFS reader) understands the livelihood model, sees that a live demonstration is **being built**, and knows that expressing interest is **not enrolment** and **not a promise of ₹1 lakh**.

---

# 2. Target Stakeholder

**Who:** Practising farmers in West Bengal; FPO / local team members; people who need to understand Integrated Farming in order to join later.

**Mindset:** “Is this a slogan on a pandal? Will I get ₹1 lakh? How do I register? Is the farm real? What do I have to put in?”

**Already know:** Paddy risk, input cost, seasonality. May not know the English letters “IFS”.

**Do not know:** That this website cannot enrol them today; that ₹1 lakh is proposed seed-support for a patron pairing, not a guaranteed farmer grant paid here; that the wetland demo is being built.

**Likely to question:** Payment, land requirement, who owns the model, government scheme, whether “join” is a lottery.

The reader must be able to:

1. Explain IFS as a loop on small land, not a poster.
2. Name the main enterprises and the circular transfers already on file.
3. Distinguish **interest** vs **enrolment** vs **approved participation**.

5. Know ₹1 lakh is **PROPOSED** seed-support in the mobilisation model — **not** a farmer grant paid on this page.
6. Download an interest note without believing they are registered in a live programme.

# 10. Section-by-Section Content Architecture

### 10.1 Why Integrated Farming

- **Heading direction:** One crop carries the whole year’s risk. A loop does not.
- **Key message:** Small holding; leftover becomes next input.
- **Status:** Explanatory. West Bengal need copy on `#ifs` may be linked as depth (fragmentation, input cost, climate, cash seasonality) without new statistics.

### 10.2 The farmer

- **Heading direction:** The annadata is the person who feeds the household.
- **Key message:** Ashtami awards for unnamed farmers; nominations no entry fee on the main site; recognition ≠ farm.

### 10.3 The loop (core ecosystem)

Must be present in V1, drawn from approved IFS content:

| Enterprise | Role on file |
|---|---|
| Crop | Cereals, pulses, oilseeds, fodder; residue to livestock/compost |
| Horticulture | Bund / homestead vegetables and trees |
| Livestock | Dairy; dung to energy/compost |
| Poultry / duckery | Beside water; duck–fish noted in model |
| Fishery / aquaculture | Pond as water + fish |
| Water harvesting | Pond as reservoir |
| Compost / bio-inputs | Residue and manure return |
| Soil | Receives compost / silt |
| Bee, mushroom | Allied livelihoods on the same holding |
| Market / household | Produce and income return |

**Circular model (do not call guaranteed zero-waste):**

- crop residue → livestock / compost  
- manure → soil / energy / fertiliser  
- pond silt → fields  
- water → crop / fishery / horticulture  
- produce → household / market  

**Forbidden:** “guaranteed zero-waste”, “guaranteed income”.

### 10.4 Model numbers

If retained from `#ifs`:

- 365-day cash flow — **ILLUSTRATIVE**
- 3×–5× income range — **ILLUSTRATIVE**
- 50–70% input-cost language — **ILLUSTRATIVE / MODEL-BASED**; not a guarantee  
- Holding sketch about 1 to 2.5 acres — **MODEL-BASED**

Always show the existing caveat: outcomes vary by farm size, location, management, crop mix, input costs, yields and market conditions.

### 10.5 FarmTech and AgriTech

On-file only: IFS pillar E — monitoring, drip, solar aeration, digital diagnostics as **illustrative model**, “where they earn their keep.”

Do not invent a FarmTech product catalogue, AI brand names, or a farmer app that does not exist.

**[STAKEHOLDER DEFINITION REQUIRED]** if the page must define FarmTech and AgriTech as a sector beyond this pillar.

### 10.6 Live farm

- East Kolkata Wetlands, 500 metres from Sector V — already on file.
- Status: **being built**. 2026 pavilion intended as working farm, not only facade.
- Whether dump-yard-like transformation = this plot: **PENDING CONFIRMATION**.
- Dispatches when posted; do not invent a feed.

### 10.7 Transformation

Shared six-stage module. Empty photographs. INTENDED. 5,000 in stage 6 remains TARGET.

### 10.8 Scale

About 5,000 village farms = mobilisation **TARGET**, Puja 2026 toward Puja 2027 as already published. Not a completed count. Patron door is separate.

### 10.9 Participate
