# 1. Document Purpose

Specify the public / visitor experience so a neighbour, family, or festival visitor meets **the Puja first** — worship, craft, food, music, neighbourhood — and then understands, without cryptic fragments, why the farmer and Integrated Farming belong in that gathering.

The Puja must not feel like an agriculture conference. Agriculture must not feel like an unrelated insert.

# 7. Relationship to Bharatiya Krishak Samaj Pujo

Mahacharya required the causal story to be explicit (not “Puja opens the door” fragments):

```text
1. The Puja is the public meeting-place (neighbourhood, clay, dhak, food, homecoming).
2. The gathering names the farmer (annadata), so the person who feeds the household is seen.
3. Recognition — Ashtami award for unnamed farmers; nominations; no entry fee.
4. Livelihood follows: Integrated Farming as a loop a small farm can run.
5. Demonstration: a live farm being built (East Kolkata Wetlands, 500 m from Sector V).
6. The Puja is also the public beginning of a mobilisation TARGET (about 5,000 farms) — not money already collected.
```

Each step **follows from the last**. The public page leads with 1–3; 4–6 are present but must not overtake worship.
