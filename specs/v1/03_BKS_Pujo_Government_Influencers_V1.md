# 10. Section-by-Section Content Architecture

### 10.1 What is being built

Sharadiya 2026, Kolkata, BKS organising partner, KarmYog organiser. Live IFS demonstration EKW 500 m from Sector V, **being built**. Aim to seed about 5,000 village farms = **TARGET**.

### 10.2 Why it matters

Use pressures already in IFS material: small/marginal holdings, input costs, flood/cyclone, seasonal cash. **Do not add new percentages.** Ask the reader to look at a demonstration they can eventually walk, and a mobilisation that needs local legitimacy.

### 10.3 Execution

Puja = public platform (neighbourhood, awards, exhibition). Farm = proof. 5,000 aim pairs village site, patron, local farmer team — **INTENDED** pairing, not a live allocation engine. Payment not taken here. Venue and committee **TO BE ANNOUNCED** where unnamed.

### 10.4 Integrated farm vision

Water, horticulture, livestock, soil, careful tools on one small farm. Demonstration being built so the model is not only a diagram.

### 10.5 FarmTech and AgriTech

Relevance: model includes monitoring / careful tools. No notified AgriTech mission, no named vendors. **[STAKEHOLDER DEFINITION REQUIRED]** for anything more.

### 10.6 Transformation

Shared module. Empty frames. Dump-yard public language **PENDING CONFIRMATION**.

### 10.7 Scale

5,000 = goal, not completed census, **not a government target adopted by any department on this page**. Patronage amounts live on the NRB door. This page does not sell packages.

### 10.8 Stakeholder role

MLAs, officers, civic voices: understand execution, visit when walkable, say what would make village-farm pairing responsible. Request for a briefing, not a campaign advertisement.

### 10.9 294 framing

West Bengal assembly **294** seats as currently on file. Scale of stakeholder universe. **Not** a list of endorsements. Do not upgrade to 295. Do not imply 294 partners.
