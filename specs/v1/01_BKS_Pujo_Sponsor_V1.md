# 1. Document Purpose

Specify the Sponsor V1 experience so a corporate decision-maker can understand the opportunity, the limits, and the actual next step — a conversation, not a four-day booking and not an online payment.

Implementation must not begin from screenshots alone. Screenshot text is evidence of the current local review, not automatically approved final content.

# 2. Target Stakeholder

**Who:** Corporate and organisational sponsors; marketing / CSR / partnership leads; FarmTech and AgriTech organisations (term directed by Ram Sir; **definition [STAKEHOLDER DEFINITION REQUIRED]**); other strategic partners.

**Mindset arriving:** “Is this a pandal hoarding for four days? What is the organisation? Who is actually in charge? What do I get? What does it cost? Is the title already taken?”

**Already know:** Durga Puja is Bengal’s public week. Many have sponsored festivals before as branding inventory.

**Do not know:** Why this Pujo names the farmer; what Integrated Farming is; that Bharatiya Krishak Samaj is organising partner not title sponsor; that KarmYog organises; that no payment happens on this website.

**Likely to question:** ROI, footfall, media, title availability, package amounts, FarmTech/AgriTech fit, three-year claim, government links, whether BKS is selling the title to itself.

# 5. V1 Objective

By the end of the page the sponsor must understand:

1. This is Bharatiya Krishak Samaj Pujo, organised by KarmYog for the 21st Century, with Bharatiya Krishak Samaj as organising partner.
2. The ask is a **sponsorship conversation**, not a four-day booking and not an online payment.
3. The story is Puja → farmer visibility → Integrated Farming → public demonstration → longer participation.
4. FarmTech and AgriTech is a relevant sector conversation **without a fabricated definition**.
5. Title sponsorship is **not occupied by BKS**; public status is **Pending confirmation** until Sir says otherwise.
6. Existing ₹10 lakh / ₹2.5 lakh class figures are **ASSUMED** unless frozen or confirmed.
7. Nothing on the page guarantees audience, media, ROI, or government partnership.
8. Expressing interest downloads a file; BKS does not automatically receive it.

# 6. Core Positioning

Approved direction (current local + six-experience spec, minus Palm Tech / 3C):

**A sponsorship conversation around Bharatiya Krishak Samaj Pujo — not a four-day booking.**

Durga Puja is when Bengal is outdoors together. This Pujo places the farmer and Integrated Farming inside that week. The intended setting is decision-makers and the public gathering in one living week. That is **INTENDED**, not a guaranteed footfall.

**Season 1 of a three-year movement** is currently on the local page and is **PENDING CONFIRMATION** as locked public language. Year 2 and Year 3 programmes are **not published**. If Sir does not confirm, V1 must soften to: a seasonal platform meant to continue; later years not published here.

# 7. Relationship to Bharatiya Krishak Samaj Pujo

```text
Durga Puja (public week)
  → Bharatiya Krishak Samaj as organising partner
  → farmer is seen (annadata, awards)
  → Integrated Farming is shown (livelihood, not slogan)
  → organisations can enter as sponsors of the platform
  → longer-term agricultural movement (only as confirmed language)
```

Sponsor value is **not** “your logo on an idol for four nights” as the whole story. It is presence at the start of a named platform. Do not invent ROI to complete that sentence.

---

# 8. Page Narrative

Recommended story sequence and why:

1. **Hero — the conversation, not the rate card.** A sponsor who lands on packages first will treat this as inventory. Lead with why this is not four days.
2. **Opportunity — enter while the platform is being named.** Title is not occupied by BKS. Do not claim “Open” unless Sir locks that word.
3. **Why different — decision-makers and the public week.** Qualitative, no invented footfall.
4. **The season — longer than four days.** Use three-year language only if confirmed; otherwise “meant to continue / later years not published.”
5. **FarmTech and AgriTech — relevance without definition theatre.** One short section. No fake market map.
6. **What the Puja / farmer / IFS have to do with a sponsor.** So agriculture is not an unrelated insert.
7. **Transformation module — proof of a continuing place.** Empty frames until photographs are approved. Honest.
8. **Organising partner — BKS is not the title.** Credential, not a sales boast.
9. **Title slot — Pending confirmation.** Dashed opportunity box, not a fake sold-out lockup and not a fake “Open” campaign unless approved.
10. **Assumed packages — only if Sir allows them to remain visible.** Clearly ASSUMED.
11. **Enquire — one primary action.** Download, not payment.
12. **FAQ / objections.** Answer what a board will actually ask.

Do not lead with NRB ₹1 lakh, farmer enrolment, or MLA pitch.

# 10. Section-by-Section Content Architecture

### 10.1 Opportunity

- **Purpose:** Name the buyer and the ask.
- **Heading direction:** Enter at the beginning of the platform.
- **Key message:** Conversation, not online payment. Title not occupied by BKS.
- **Evidence:** Credential strip; title slot later on the page.
- **CTA:** Express Sponsor Interest.
- **Status:** INTENDED platform. Title PENDING CONFIRMATION.

### 10.2 Why different

- **Purpose:** Contrast conference vs mass event vs this Pujo week.
- **Heading direction:** Decision-makers and the public gathering, in one living week.
- **Key message:** Intended setting, not guaranteed footfall / media / income.
- **Evidence:** None invented. Qualitative only.
- **Status:** INTENDED.

### 10.3 The season

- **Purpose:** Not four days.
- **Heading:** If confirmed: Season 1 of a three-year movement. If not: A seasonal platform meant to continue.
- **Key message:** Year 2 / Year 3 programmes are **not published**. Not a television reality show.
- **Status:** Three-year slogan **PENDING CONFIRMATION**. Later years **NOT YET AVAILABLE**.

### 10.4 FarmTech and AgriTech

- **Purpose:** Sector relevance without Palm Tech / 3C.
- **Heading direction:** Relevant to FarmTech and AgriTech — terms as directed; definition not invented.
- **Key message:** This Pujo shows Integrated Farming, including careful tools already described in the IFS model (monitoring, drip, energy where they earn their keep). That is **MODEL-BASED**. It is not a named product marketplace and not a list of confirmed sector partners.
- **If more definition is demanded:** **[STAKEHOLDER DEFINITION REQUIRED]**
- **Forbidden:** Invented TAM, named companies, “first-time platform for [undefined acronyms].”

### 10.5 Puja → farmer → IFS (short)

- **Purpose:** So a sponsor understands why agriculture sits inside the festival.
- **Key message:** Use the explicit causal chain (Mahacharya): gathering → farmer seen → recognition → livelihood (IFS) → live demonstration being built → larger mobilisation as **TARGET**.
- **Do not** use cryptic “opens the door” fragments.

### 10.6 Transformation

- **Purpose:** Continuing place, not four-day set.
- **Key message:** INTENDED story. Frames empty until photographs approved. Dump-yard wording **PENDING CONFIRMATION**. Whether this is the East Kolkata Wetlands plot is **PENDING CONFIRMATION**.
- **CTA:** See the live farm (honest: being built).
- **Status:** D / empty slots.

### 10.7 Organising partner

- **Purpose:** BKS is partner, not title.
- **Heading:** Bharatiya Krishak Samaj.
- **Key message:** Organising partner. Does not occupy the title-sponsor slot.
- **Evidence:** Address already on file.

### 10.8 Title sponsorship

- **Purpose:** Vacant commercial slot, honestly labelled.
- **Heading:** Title sponsorship opportunity.
- **Value:** **Pending confirmation** (current). Do not switch to **Open** without Sir.
- **Forbidden:** Implying BKS bought or holds the title. Implying a sponsor is already appointed.

### 10.9 Assumed packages

- **Purpose:** Do not hide what the holding campaign page already publishes — unless Sir freezes them.
- **Content if visible:** Title Sponsor ₹10 lakh ASSUMED; Award Category Sponsor ₹2.5 lakh ASSUMED. Other campaign amounts remain on the holding site, not as new claims here.
- **If frozen:** Remove numbers; say commercial amounts are being finalised; conversation still welcome.
- **Status:** REQUIRES STAKEHOLDER DECISION.

### 10.10 Enquire

- See §§13–14.
