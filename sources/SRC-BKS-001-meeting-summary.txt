Overview

The team aligned on a Durga Puja launch centered on the Krishak Samaj theme, using the BKS platform and KarmYog’s work to mobilize the agricultural community around integrated farming systems. The meeting set a crowdfunding goal to seed-fund 5,000 farmers across West Bengal and outlined immediate needs around research content and improving an AI voice agent for locality-to-booth mapping.

Durga Puja Theme, Platform Experience, and Narrative
Durga Puja theme: Krishak Samaj, showcasing an end-to-end experience on the BKS platform tied to KarmYog’s mobilization of the agricultural community toward integrated farming systems.
Integrated farming system framing to include a holistic model (e.g., agro-tourism, retail, storage, and full ecosystem experience).
Research & Website Content (BKS West Bengal)
Requirement to conduct deep research on integrated farming in West Bengal and identify best practices.
Output to be published on the BKS West Bengal website as core reference content supporting the Durga Puja campaign narrative.
Crowdfunding Plan & Targets
Plan to use AI to mobilize participation for an integrated farming pilot via crowdfunding.
Coverage target: 295 constituencies, ~15 people per constituency (~5,000 farmers).
Funding target: 1 lakh seed funding per farmer (total goal aligned to funding 5,000 farmers).
Donor audience: NRIs and donors across India and globally.
Launch timing: by Durga Puja.
AI/Voice Agent & Geo-Hierarchy Mapping
A voice agent has an initial version; it needs refinement to work reliably at every area level.
Key mapping challenge: people generally know district (e.g., Murshidabad) and may know Zilla Parishad/Parishad district and sometimes assembly constituency, but typically do not know their polling booth.
Needed capability: guide users from known locality/area names (e.g., “Jatragachi,” “Shalda”) through the hierarchy to identify the correct polling booth.
Action items
Do deep research on integrated farming in West Bengal, compile best practices, and prepare content for the BKS West Bengal website.
Fine-tune the existing map so it works accurately for every area/locality level.
Improve the voice agent to map users from locality/area -> district/Zilla Parishad -> assembly constituency -> polling booth (handling cases where users don’t know constituency/booth but know their area name).
